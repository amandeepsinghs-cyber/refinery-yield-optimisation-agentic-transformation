# Refinery Analytics Use Cases

## High-value use cases by value area

| Unit / process | Analytics use case | Value area | Indicative reported benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| FCC / RFCC / INDMAX | Product-quality inferential (e.g. HGO sulphur soft sensor) to run closer to plan and avoid over- and under-treating | Yield & quality | ~$0.4–0.5M/yr per unit |
| Catalytic reformer | Stabiliser-tower overhead optimisation to maximise C5 recovery | Yield & quality | ~$2–3M/yr gross-margin uplift |
| LPG / LSR naphtha system | LPG balance and distillation-split optimisation (C4/C5 split); improved forecasting | Yield & quality | ~$2–2.5M/yr |
| Reactor regeneration (CCR / hydroprocessing) | Regeneration-cycle tracking, optimisation and event-based root-cause analysis | Yield & quality | ~1% reduction in cycle-time/yield losses (~$0.7M/yr+) |
| Fired heaters / furnaces | CO and O₂ combustion modelling; automatic flagging of poor-combustion episodes | Energy | Fuel savings + CO₂/SOₓ/NOₓ reduction; ~$0.4–1M/yr per site |
| Multi-unit / multi-refinery utilities | Energy-management dashboards (boilers, furnaces, steam balance, O₂ control) | Energy | >$10M/yr at multi-refinery scale |
| Crude preheat trains / heat exchangers | UA-based fouling health signal with degradation tracking and cleaning/shutdown optimisation | Energy & reliability | Sustained heat recovery; condition-based cleaning |
| Filtration systems (alkylation, amine, hydroprocessing) | Filter/coalescer breakthrough prediction to avoid fouling and unplanned changeouts | Reliability | Avoided changeouts and outages (~$2M/yr+) |
| Rotating equipment across sites (compressors, pumps) | Asset-health monitoring and predictive maintenance scaled across refineries | Reliability | Avoided downtime + planning gains (order $1–9M) |
| Crude-unit furnaces | Coke-buildup and hydraulic-constraint prediction; anomaly detection; turnaround vs mid-run planning | Reliability | ~1% margin / ~3% production uplift + lower maintenance |
| Product soft sensors | Online property prediction between lab samples (e.g. ATF freezing point, bitumen viscosity, VR penetration) | Yield & quality | Earlier off-spec detection; reduced reprocessing |

## Downstream refinery use-case catalogue

### Separation
- Salt-deposition monitoring in crude and overhead systems.
- Vapour-cut / product-cut optimisation.
- Heat-exchanger maintenance prediction.
- Furnace de-coke monitoring and prediction.

### Reaction and conversion
- Hydrogen and fuel-gas balances.
- Fixed-bed catalyst life prediction.
- Asset-based calculations for conversion assets (alkylation reactors, coker drums, etc.).
- Compressor health monitoring.

### Treating
- Predict product quality from upstream operating conditions.
- Filter / drier cycle optimisation.
- Amine (DEA) system monitoring.
- Chemical-additive optimisation.

### Scheduling and planning
- Blend-giveaway optimisation.
- Inventory monitoring and prediction.
- Material-balance monitoring.
- Feedstock evaluation.

### Workforce empowerment
- Process-engineering / morning shift reporting.
- Start-up procedure monitoring.
- Loss tracking and categorisation.
- Control-loop performance monitoring.

### Sustainability and compliance
- Automated regulatory reporting.
- Flare and release calculations.
- Furnace NOₓ emissions prediction.
- Emissions calculations during analyser / detector exceedances.

### Pipeline and product movement
- Leak detection and monitoring.
- Pigging-schedule prediction.
- Drag-reducing-agent (DRA) optimisation.
- Pump and valve performance (valve throttling, pump-seal maintenance scheduling).
- Custody / meter monitoring at scale.
- Line-pressure optimisation.
