## 4 Root cause

5-Whys Analysis:
1. Why did actual LCO T98 drift high? Controller increased tray heating based on falsely low temperature readings.
2. Why was the temperature reading low? Type-K thermocouple junction suffered metallurgical embrittlement.
3. Why did the single-sensor alarm fail to trip? Sensor drifted within allowable operating bounds without open-circuit failure.
4. Why was analytical cross-check delayed? 8-hour lab testing turnaround permitted 6 hours of unobserved drift.
5. How could this be prevented? Multivariate sensor-health scoring in the AI soft sensor can flag collinearity breakdowns immediately.
