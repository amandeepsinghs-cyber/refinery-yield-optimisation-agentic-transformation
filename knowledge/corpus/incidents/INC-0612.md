## 4 Root cause

5-Whys Analysis:
1. Why did the unit experience a 0.6% yield degradation? Cut-point was set 8 °F below optimal plan target.
2. Why did operators lower the cut-point? Fear of exceeding 765 °F critical threshold without measurement visibility.
3. Why was there no measurement visibility? Laboratory analyzer was offline for 8 hours.
4. Why was secondary backup unavailable? Unit had no secondary physical distillation analyzer.
5. Why was there no virtual measurement? Soft-sensor advisory system had not yet completed validation.

## 5 Contributing factors

Conservative operational posture induced by lack of real-time inferential measurement redundancy.
