## 1 Summary

On 2025-04-09, following a planned transition from baseline API 25.0 crude to heavier API 21.0 crude, LCO distillation endpoint LCO_T98_F exceeded the critical high specification of 765 °F, peaking at 771.2 °F for 3.5 hours before corrective manual intervention.

## 2 Timeline

- 04:00 Feed changeover initiated per [SOP-FRAC-002 r5 §4.2]; feed API tag dist_feed_API ramped from 25.0 to 21.0 over 60 minutes.
- 05:30 Column heat balance shifted; tray 13 draw temperature T_tray13_F climbed from 476.4 °F to 488.1 °F.
- 06:00 Routine morning lab draw collected; sample delivery delayed by 45 minutes.
- 07:15 LCO_T98_F breached critical threshold of 765 °F, reaching peak 771.2 °F.
- 10:45 Laboratory analysis results received; board operator immediately raised MV_PA2 duty and trimmed cut point.
- 14:15 Product distillation re-established within specification at 756.0 °F.

## 4 Root cause

5-Whys Analysis:
1. Why did LCO T98 exceed 765 °F? Heavier feed slate produced higher boiling fractionator bottoms vapor.
2. Why was heat removal inadequate? Intermediate pumparound duty was held at static baseline settings.
3. Why did operators not trim pumparound earlier? Operators depended on delayed laboratory confirmation.
4. Why was the lab result delayed? 12-hour sampling cycle and sample transport latency created a 5-hour blindspot.
5. Why was there no real-time inferential tracking? Unit lacked verified online soft-sensor inferentials.

## 5 Contributing factors

Absence of automated advisory inferentials and inadequate fast-loop pumparound compensation during feed transition.

## 6 Corrective and preventive actions

1. Increase routine lab sampling frequency from 12 h to 8 h via [MOC-0124 r1 §1] (Owner: Grace Mwangi, Due: 2025-11-10).
